For business buyers

Useful details for a faster quote.

When you contact us, include as many of these details as possible.

What should I send?

Product type, quantity, size range, fabric preference, artwork/logo, reference photos and delivery country.

Can you make private-label apparel?

Tell us your label, logo, labels and packaging needs so we can confirm what is feasible for your order.

What about minimum order quantity?

MOQ depends on the garment, fabric, printing and production requirements. Ask us for the MOQ for your project.

Do you ship internationally?

Share your destination country and required delivery terms so export and shipping options can be discussed.
